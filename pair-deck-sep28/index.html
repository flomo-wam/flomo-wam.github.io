Part 1 · World action models
Does a WAM need to generate video at test time?
Fast-WAM compares three designs on the same backbone.
  • Joint, video-then-IDM and training-only video score about the same
  • Removing the video loss from training costs the most
  • Without video at test time, inference is 3–4× faster
Fast-WAM, arXiv 2603.16666, Fig. 1

Part 2 · Semantic features
VTP: semantic losses when training the tokenizer
HowTrain the tokenizer with reconstruction plus self-supervised and CLIP losses
ResultGeneration keeps improving with tokenizer compute (FID −65.8% at 10×); with reconstruction only it stalls
For usAdd a semantic loss when we train the flow encoder
VTP (MiniMax), arXiv 2512.13687, Fig. 1
31 / 44
Part 2 · Semantic features
RepWAM: an aligned tokenizer inside a WAM
HowTokenizer latents are aligned to a frozen Perception Encoder; the WAM trains on these tokens
ResultSame 1.3B WAM on RoboTwin 2.0: 86.6 vs 78.0 with the Wan VAE
For usAlign flow-encoder latents to DINO features of the matching RGB frames
RepWAM, arXiv 2606.13674, Fig. 1 and Table 3
32 / 44
Part 2 · Semantic features
REPA: align the DiT, not the tokenizer
HowAn MLP maps an early DiT layer onto frozen DINOv2 features of the clean image
ResultSame image quality with more than 17.5× fewer training steps
For usAlign FloMo’s DiT features to DINO; the encoders stay as they are
REPA, arXiv 2410.06940, ICLR 2025, Fig. 1
