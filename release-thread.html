3D Scene Flow as a World-Action Model Intermediate for Learning from Human Video

  1. 01

    1 / 10 · Thesis

    [1/10] World-action models usually predict future RGB. But robot actions depend on how a scene moves—not on reconstructing every texture and reflection. FloMo asks: what if a video-pretrained policy predicted 3D scene flow + actions instead of pixels? 🧵
  2. 02

    2 / 10 · Representation

    [2/10] What is 3D scene flow? For each tracked point, FloMo predicts its cumulative 3D displacement in the first camera frame. Unlike RGB, it discards appearance. Unlike 2D flow, it separates task motion from camera ego-motion. 3D scene flow becomes the shared language.

    RGB observation · 2D point tracks · 3D scene flow

  3. 03

    3 / 10 · Architecture

    [3/10] How can a video model predict 3D scene flow? FloMo renders scene flow as RGB, encodes it with the frozen Wan 2.2 VAE, and LoRA-tunes its 5B DiT to jointly flow-match scene flow + robot actions. No new tokenizer: it reuses the pretrained video latent space.
  4. 04

    4 / 10 · Training

    [4/10] Training has two stages: 1) Human video supervises scene flow; robot data supervises scene flow + action, building a broad prior. 2) Robot-only fine-tuning sharpens action prediction. At test time, FloMo plans 16-action chunks from an image + instruction.

  5. 05

    5 / 10 · In-distribution

    [5/10] Does the target matter? On Oven, Toolbox, and Corn, FloMo is best on every task. Average in-distribution success: • FloMo: 87% • Joint Video-Action: 47% • FloMo + Video: 40% • BC from scratch: 7% Predicting 3D scene flow beats predicting pixels.
  6. 06

    6 / 10 · OOD generalization

    [6/10] The gap widens OOD. FloMo leads on an unseen motion primitive, task, and object: • Close Cabinet: 5/5 • Pull String: 3/5 • Highlighter: 4/5 Pixel-predicting methods degrade sharply. A 3D scene-flow intermediate transfers beyond the robot training distribution.

  7. 07

    7 / 10 · Sample efficiency

    [7/10] Why does 3D scene flow work? In controlled inverse-dynamics probes, scene flow reaches the same action-prediction ceiling as video despite containing less information—and with 1% of the data, it yields 22% lower action error. The useful signal is simpler to learn.
  8. 08

    8 / 10 · Attention

    [8/10] When RGB and 3D scene flow are both available, what does the action stream use? Across the DiT, action attention shifts toward scene flow, averaging ~3× more attention to 3D scene flow than video. The model itself seeks out the action-relevant representation.
  9. 09

    9 / 10 · Human-video transfer

    [9/10] Human video is where OOD transfer comes from. Human hands and robot grippers create similar 3D scene flow. Remove ~81h of action-free human video—same architecture and target—and average OOD success falls from 80% to 7%. 3D scene flow makes human motion reusable.

  10. 10

    10 / 10 · Takeaway

    [10/10] Pull String appears only in human video. Robot success: FloMo 3/5; DreamZero 1/5; BC 0/5. Video pretraining shouldn’t mean predicting more pixels. 3D scene flow lets human video teach robots how the world moves—and how to act. https://flomo-wam.github.io
